Variational raises $110 million at $1.5 billion valuation

Variational raised $110 million at a $1.5 billion valuation in a Bain Capital Crypto-led Series B, funding more markets, higher position limits and 24/7 swaps.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 20, 2026 · 03:28 UTC2 min read

Variational raised $110 million at a $1.5 billion valuation in a Series B led by Bain Capital Crypto on Nov. 18, the company said in its funding announcement. Dragonfly, HSG, Peak XV and 50T Funds also participated.

Funding targets more markets and higher limits

Variational said it will use the capital to raise open interest caps on Omni, its on-chain derivatives platform, and add hundreds of stock, index, commodity and foreign-exchange listings. The company also plans to expand swaps, which it launched in September with liquidity sourced from traditional financial institutions.

Omni has processed more than $475 billion in trading volume across over 100,000 accounts since its 2025 launch, according to Variational. The company said it has accumulated more than $1.3 billion in open interest, with traditional markets accounting for more than half of daily volume.

Omni uses a market maker to quote prices

Variational’s model uses an in-house liquidity provider to take the other side of trades and quote prices using liquidity from crypto exchanges and traditional financial firms, CoinDesk reported. The company says this approach supports more than 590 markets, spanning crypto and traditional assets.

Variational said its swaps have generated more than $80 billion in volume since launching. Unlike perpetual futures, the swaps use financing rates tied to traditional markets, according to CoinDesk.

More listings and 24-hour swaps planned for 2026

Variational said its plans for the rest of 2026 include raising position limits, listing more swap markets, launching a public trading API and moving swaps toward 24-hour trading. The company also plans to expand its network of institutional counterparties able to quote U.S. and global equities, commodities and currency pairs.
