Crypto Yield Portfolio: A Monthly Vault Routine for Holders

Build a hands off crypto yield portfolio with auto compounding vaults by splitting assets across risk tiers, depositing once and reviewing status monthly.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 03:54 UTC2 min read

A hands-off crypto yield portfolio starts with a few auto-compounding vaults, split by risk and reviewed monthly. Vaults harvest and reinvest rewards, but you still choose allocations and decide when to exit.

Have ready a wallet with assets on one chain, its gas token, and a chosen allocation. Pick a chain you already use, such as Arbitrum, Base or Polygon, and confirm the assets you plan to allocate are ready there.

Build around three risk tiers

Split capital across stablecoin, blue-chip single-asset and optional liquidity-pool (LP) vaults. Single-asset vaults avoid impermanent loss; LP vaults carry it because the value of the deposited tokens can shift relative to holding them separately.

Keep LPs as a minority share if you use them: their potential yield comes with that added risk. Spread the rest across different underlying protocols, since choosing several vaults alone may still leave the portfolio relying on the same protocol.

Auto-compounding vaults reinvest harvested rewards without requiring you to make each reinvestment transaction. The Beefy app is a multichain yield optimizer for supported DeFi strategies; the Beefy app can provide the interface for depositing into vaults that match your tiers.

Set up the positions and review monthly

Use this routine to turn the allocation into positions you can monitor:

  1. Connect your wallet to the Beefy app. The wallet is connected and ready to select vaults.
  2. Filter vaults by chain and asset for each tier. You have a short list that matches your chosen allocation.
  3. Deposit the planned amount into each tier’s vault. Each allocation becomes a separate vault position.
  4. Note the mooTokens received and each vault’s price per share. These give you a record to compare at your next review.
  5. Set a monthly review for vault status, APY and exit options. At each review, confirm the positions still fit your plan and compare price per share with your notes.

The task is running when price per share has risen between reviews and the vaults remain active. APY can change, so treat it as a current reference rather than a promise of future returns.

Check vault status every month: the Beefy app marks retired vaults as EOL, and an EOL vault stops compounding, leaving funds idle until you withdraw them. Group positions by underlying protocol during the same review; that catches hidden concentration and saves you from mistaking several vault names for broad diversification.
