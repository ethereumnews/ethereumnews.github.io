About Crypto Execution Weekly

We report on the machinery behind crypto trades: matching engines, routers, collateral systems and settlement paths. Our reporting follows what builders ship, how traders use it and what breaks under load. We judge releases by their effect on fills, costs and access to liquidity.

We are editorially independent; projects and advertisers cannot buy coverage or approve copy. We source claims from code changes, documentation, transaction records, reproducible tests and interviews. We distinguish our measurements from vendor claims and disclose relevant conflicts. We correct factual errors promptly and explain substantive changes in dated notes.
