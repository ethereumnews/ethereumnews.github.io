About Forkline

Forkline covers Ethereum and the wider crypto infrastructure around it: protocol upgrades, scaling networks, market structure, governance, and on-chain finance. The publication focuses on useful facts and clear context rather than token promotion or daily price noise.

Mara Ellison is Forkline’s managing editor. She has reported on Ethereum scaling and digital-asset markets since 2018, including research work on rollups, validator economics, and decentralized exchanges. Her reporting starts with primary documents, public data, and direct statements from project teams.

Forkline separates reporting from opinion and identifies uncertainty when the facts are still developing. The goal is a compact, reliable record of what is changing across crypto’s settlement layer.
