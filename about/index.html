About Web3 Hub

Web3 Hub is an independent news outlet covering the crypto industry as a whole: Bitcoin, Ethereum and other networks, exchanges, DeFi, stablecoins, regulation, security incidents and protocol upgrades. No project, token or company owns or directs our coverage.

Every report is built from primary sources — filings, on-chain data, official statements and published documents — and links to them where possible. When we get something wrong, we correct it on the article and note the change. Web3 Hub is independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
