How to split ETH across staking, liquid staking and restaking

How to spread ETH across staking, liquid staking and restaking: keep a plain ETH reserve, stake the rest, and restake only the share you can risk.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 25, 2026 · 19:21 UTC2 min read

Write down a plain ETH reserve, a staked share and a smaller restaked share, then confirm both positions on-chain.

What should you have ready?

Have a backed-up self-custody wallet and enough plain ETH for gas before you start. Set the reserve amount by what you may need to spend or withdraw; there is no fixed percentage that suits every holder.

The layers are nested, not separate buckets. Restaked ETH is already staked ETH, so the decision is how much of your staked share you are willing to expose to additional slashing risk from the services being secured.

That choice depends on whether you want direct restaking, an LRT or no extra layer. As a next step, read our take on choosing an eigen layer route to compare those options before committing more ETH.

How should you split and set up each share?

Choose the staked amount after setting the reserve, then decide what fraction of that stake you can accept putting at additional risk.

  1. Set the amounts. Write down reserve, staked and restaked amounts in ETH. The restaked amount must be part of the staked amount, not added on top of it. The visible result is a split you can check against your wallet balance.
  2. Stake the chosen share. Below 32 ETH, you can stake through a liquid staking token such as stETH from Lido or rETH from Rocket Pool. At 32 ETH, you can run a validator; a solo validator requires 32 ETH. After the transaction, your wallet should show the LST balance, or your validator position if you chose native staking.
  3. Restake only the amount you chose. Deposit that share of your LST into eigen layer through its strategy contracts, then delegate it to an operator you have selected. Native staked ETH can enter through an EigenPod instead. The app should show the deposit and the operator; restaking adds potential rewards and AVS slashing risk.
  4. Confirm the positions. Check the wallet for the LST balance and the eigen layer app for the deposit and delegated operator. The task is done when those on-chain positions match your written split.
