EU proposes crypto crackdown in 22nd Russia sanctions package

The European Commission proposed restrictions on Russian banks and crypto networks on Nov. 23, but all 27 EU states must approve the package before it takes effect.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 23, 2026 · 22:14 UTC2 min read

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen proposed targeting 60 Russian banks and tightening restrictions on crypto and payment networks on Nov. 23, as part of the EU’s 22nd Russia sanctions package, according to the Associated Press report.

The Commission’s statement on the proposed package says the 60 banks would face an extended transaction ban and be removed from SWIFT, the international financial messaging system. The proposal would also target crypto and payment networks in Russia and in third countries that help Russia evade sanctions.

Von der Leyen said the EU had launched a Crypto Sanctions Task Force to support that work. The statement did not name crypto platforms or specify how many firms or networks would be affected.

The package also proposes adding more than 70 vessels to the list of Russia’s shadow fleet and banning Russian-insured tankers, alongside wider energy service restrictions. For sporting events in the EU, the Commission proposed barring Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag, banning official Russian symbols and restricting sponsor funding to European sports and cultural groups.

The measures remain proposals. All 27 EU member states must endorse them before they can take effect, and the approval process can take weeks, the AP reported. The package may change during that process.
