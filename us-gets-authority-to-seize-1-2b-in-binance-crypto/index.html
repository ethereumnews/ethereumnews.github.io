US Gets Authority to Seize $1.2B in Binance Crypto

The Justice Department says courts authorized seizure or restraint of more than $1.2 billion from the Binance theft as US efforts to recover funds continue.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 18, 2026 · 21:38 UTC2 min read

The Justice Department said Nov. 12 that it had obtained court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion in cryptocurrency stolen from Binance. The department’s announcement said the assets were part of a theft carried out on or about Oct. 29 by North Korean military hackers it calls TraderTraitor.

The department said US agencies and private-sector partners have frozen more than $2.7 billion of the stolen assets so far. The authorization covers more than $1.2 billion of that frozen total; the announcement describes the action as seizure or restraint, rather than saying the funds have been forfeited.

The Associated Press reported the FBI’s announcement and said the theft was about $6.3 billion. The FBI had attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, according to AP. The Justice Department described the stolen property as virtual assets taken from the Binance cryptocurrency exchange.

Authorities are working with virtual asset service providers to trace and block the funds, the Justice Department said. The hackers continue to launder assets through cryptocurrency bridges, mixers and exchanges, and the FBI has urged service providers to block transactions linked to addresses used in that activity.

Seizure authorization does not by itself determine who ultimately receives recovered assets. AP reported that the government typically files a forfeiture claim with a judge, who then decides who has a legitimate claim to the funds. The Justice Department said efforts to trace, freeze, seize and forfeit the Binance assets are ongoing.
