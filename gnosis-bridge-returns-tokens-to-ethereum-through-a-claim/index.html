Gnosis Bridge returns tokens to Ethereum through a claim

How to bridge tokens from Gnosis Chain to Ethereum: submit the return transfer, wait for validation, claim on Ethereum and verify the recipient balance.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 17, 2026 · 02:06 UTC2 min read

Use Gnosis Bridge to send the withdrawal from Gnosis Chain, wait for validation, then claim it on Ethereum and verify the recipient balance. The Gnosis Bridge handles the return flow, which separates the initial transfer from execution of the claim on Ethereum. The Gnosis to Ethereum bridge is complete only when that claim succeeds.

How do you send and claim the withdrawal?

Complete the return in order: choose a supported token and recipient, initiate the transfer, track its status, then claim and check the balance.

  1. Prepare your wallet and funds. Start with the token you want to return in your wallet on Gnosis Chain. Have xDAI available there and ETH in your Ethereum wallet. xDAI is used on Gnosis Chain, while Ethereum ETH is needed for the Ethereum transaction.
  2. Configure the transfer. Select the supported token, choose Ethereum as the destination and enter the recipient address. Confirm that the address is for the wallet where you want to receive the tokens. For a token such as WETH, select it only if it is available as a supported option for the transfer.
  3. Authorize and initiate. Approve the token when your wallet prompts you, then submit the transfer. Save the transaction hash shown for the Gnosis Chain transaction. That hash is the reference you will need to follow the transfer and recover it later.
  4. Track validation. Search for the transaction hash in the Gnosis Bridge Explorer. Its Unclaimed status means sufficient signatures have been collected for an Ethereum claim. At that point, switch your wallet to Ethereum and submit Claim.
  5. Verify receipt. After the Ethereum claim succeeds, check the recipient wallet’s balance for the expected token. The return is finished when the balance reflects the claimed amount.

Note: Unclaimed means the transfer is ready to claim; it does not mean the tokens have already arrived on Ethereum. The claim is a separate Ethereum transaction, so keep ETH available in that wallet to submit it.

How can you resume a withdrawal later?

Use the original Gnosis Chain transaction hash to find an unclaimed return, even after closing the browser. Open the Gnosis Bridge Explorer and search by that hash to recover the transfer’s status. When it shows Unclaimed, connect the intended Ethereum wallet, submit Claim and verify the recipient balance. Keep the hash until the Ethereum receipt is confirmed; it lets you pick up the pending withdrawal without starting another transfer.
