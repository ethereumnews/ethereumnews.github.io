Byreal in 6 Steps: Swap, Add Liquidity and Trade Markets

Byreal’s six-step flow covers wallet setup, token swaps, concentrated liquidity and market selection, with checks on quotes, price ranges and trading risk.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomOct 21, 2026 · 12:58 UTC3 min read

Byreal lets Solana users swap tokens, add liquidity to price-ranged pools and trade markets using wallet-authorized transactions. Its six-step flow starts with a compatible wallet and ends with checking the position or trade after it settles.

Keep some SOL available for network fees, and confirm token mint addresses before trading. Byreal describes its swap routing as drawing on aggregated liquidity; the byreal interface shows a quote to review before you authorize a transaction.

How do you swap on byreal?

To swap, choose a token pair, enter an amount and review the expected output before signing. These first three steps help you check what the transaction is set to do.

  • Step 1: Connect a Solana wallet you control and make sure it holds the tokens you plan to use, plus SOL for fees.
  • Step 2: Select the token you will pay and the token you want to receive, then enter the amount.
  • Step 3: Check the quote, price impact and slippage tolerance. Byreal’s slippage documentation says a swap can revert if the price moves beyond the tolerance you set; sign only when the displayed terms suit you.

How do you add liquidity on byreal?

To provide liquidity, select a pool and set the price range where you want your tokens to be available. Byreal uses concentrated liquidity, so your deposit is focused within that range rather than spread across every possible price.

Step 4: Review the pool’s fee tier, range and required token amounts, then authorize the deposit. Byreal’s documentation says a position earns fees while the market price is within its range; if price moves outside it, the position becomes inactive and stops earning those fees until price returns. A narrower range concentrates capital more, but is more likely to go inactive as price moves.

Which markets can you trade on byreal?

Byreal lists spot swaps, perpetual contracts, prediction markets and tokenized assets, whose terms and risks differ. Check the product’s settlement details before placing an order.

Step 5: Choose the market type and asset, and review its liquidity and trading terms. For a perpetual contract, check margin, funding costs and liquidation rules; for a prediction market, check how the outcome is resolved.

Step 6: Confirm the order details in your wallet, then check the transaction status and resulting balance or liquidity position. The key distinction is that swaps exchange tokens, while liquidity deposits expose capital to a pool’s price range and market movements. Before the next trade or deposit, review the current quote or pool range again.
