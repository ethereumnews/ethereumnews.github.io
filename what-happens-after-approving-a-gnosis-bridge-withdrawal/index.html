What happens after approving a Gnosis Bridge withdrawal?

What happens after approving a token bridge withdrawal? Approval sets an ERC-20 allowance; submit a separate bridge transaction, then track the source and destination.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 17, 2026 · 16:45 UTC3 min read

After the ERC-20 approval confirms, review the withdrawal details and submit a separate bridge transaction; approval only lets the bridge contract use the specified amount. For Gnosis Bridge, follow that source transaction through processing and confirm the destination receipt or token balance before treating the withdrawal as complete.

  • Approval gives the bridge contract an ERC-20 allowance; the tokens stay in your wallet.
  • A separate source-chain transaction submits the withdrawal.
  • Track the source receipt and destination status.

Approval sets permission, not a withdrawal

An ERC-20 approval sets an allowance: the token contract records which spender may use the token and up to what amount. In this case, the spender is the bridge contract. Confirming the approval in MetaMask changes that permission, but it does not send the tokens to the bridge.

That distinction can leave the withdrawal form ready but the withdrawal itself unsubmitted. The approval has its own transaction and confirmation; your tokens remain in your wallet until you submit and confirm the separate bridge transaction.

Gnosis Bridge moves tokens between Gnosis Chain and Ethereum, so its route is a concrete example of a transfer that needs a source transaction before destination receipt. For a closer look at bridging Gnosis Chain to Ethereum with Gnosis Bridge, the route’s token movement shows why approval and withdrawal are separate steps.

The source transaction starts the bridge process

With MetaMask connected to Gnosis Chain, review the token, amount and Ethereum recipient in the bridge interface. Make sure the recipient address is correct, then submit the withdrawal and confirm that transaction in MetaMask. This is the transaction that starts the bridge process.

For instance, say you approve an amount of an ERC-20 on Gnosis Chain for a wallet on Ethereum. After the approval confirms, the token may still appear in your Gnosis Chain wallet because you have not submitted the withdrawal. Submit it, then use the source transaction receipt to follow the transfer’s progress in the interface.

GnosisScan can show transaction receipts on Gnosis Chain and Ethereum. Use it to inspect the source receipt, and check the bridge interface for destination status while the route completes its required processing. A confirmed source transaction alone does not confirm that the destination tokens have arrived.

Once the interface shows a destination transaction, or the token balance appears on Ethereum, treat the withdrawal as complete. Then use the received balance for the task you planned on Ethereum; checking the destination receipt or balance confirms it reached the right place.
