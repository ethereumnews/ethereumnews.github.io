Mantle withdrawals need a proof, a wait and an Ethereum claim

Mantle withdrawals wait for Ethereum to verify a validity proof, then need a separate claim. Plan for processing time and keep ETH ready for the final gas fee.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomSep 29, 20262 min read

Withdrawing from Mantle to Ethereum means submitting a request on Mantle, waiting for its state to be verified on Ethereum, then sending a separate claim transaction. The wait is for proof production and settlement, not the seven-day challenge period described in older guides: Mantle’s current documentation says verified batches finalize without one.

What happens when you withdraw from Mantle?

Your wallet first signs a withdrawal on Mantle, where the transaction uses MNT for gas. The bridge records the request, but the funds do not appear on Ethereum immediately; the relevant Mantle state must be covered by a validity proof verified there.

When comparing a direct exit with other transfer paths, Mantle Bridge route choices can help explain the trade-offs. A liquidity route may deliver funds sooner by paying from assets already on Ethereum, but it adds provider fees and a separate set of contract and liquidity risks.

After the proof is verified, the withdrawal becomes eligible for its final step: claiming on Ethereum. That claim is a separate transaction signed from the same wallet, and it requires ETH for Ethereum gas—even when the asset being withdrawn is ETH.

How long should you plan to wait?

Allow time for Mantle to produce and submit the proof, for Ethereum to verify it, and for the bridge interface to show the request as ready to claim. The current process has no fixed seven-day challenge window, but proof production and network congestion can still affect how long the full exit takes.

Use the withdrawal status in the bridge history as your guide rather than treating a pending balance as a failed transfer. Save the Mantle transaction hash so you can find the original request and check its progress; a wallet balance alone may not show which stage it has reached.

Unlike a deposit, which can be recognized on the destination network after the source transaction is processed, a withdrawal has this additional proof and claim sequence. That makes the canonical route a better fit when following the protocol’s settlement process matters more than receiving funds immediately.

What should you prepare before withdrawing?

Keep gas on both networks and confirm which token will arrive on Ethereum before signing. MNT pays for the Mantle transaction, while ETH pays for the later Ethereum claim; funds waiting in the bridge cannot cover that claim fee.

  • Check that the wallet is connected to Mantle and the receiving address is correct.
  • Keep enough MNT on Mantle to submit the withdrawal.
  • Keep ETH on Ethereum for the claim and allow for changing gas costs.
  • Save the transaction hash and return to the same bridge history to finish the request.

Before signing, check the route, token and destination address in the bridge interface. A confirmed withdrawal request cannot be completed by starting another one; once the existing request shows it is ready, switch the wallet to Ethereum and submit its claim.
