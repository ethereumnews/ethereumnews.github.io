How to Withdraw ETH from an Exchange to Your Wallet Before Base

To withdraw ETH from an exchange to a self-custody wallet, choose Ethereum, verify the address and fee, approve the transfer, then track it on Etherscan.

By Web3 Hub NewsroomNov 27, 2026 · 23:32 UTC2 min read

ETH on an exchange can be withdrawn to a self-custody wallet by choosing Ethereum as the network, pasting your wallet’s receive address, confirming the withdrawal and checking the transaction on Etherscan.

The exchange sends a normal Ethereum transfer to that address. You need a self-custody wallet set up and an exchange account with withdrawals enabled.

Withdraw ETH to your wallet

  1. Open the receive screen in your wallet and copy its Ethereum address. Use the address shown there, not one from transaction history or a chat. Ethereum addresses are 42 characters and start with 0x; compare the pasted address with the one in your wallet before proceeding.

  2. Open the ETH withdrawal flow on the exchange and select Ethereum as the network. The fee and minimum shown apply to the selected asset and network, and exchanges set their own withdrawal fees.

  3. Paste the wallet address and enter the amount. Review the destination address, selected network, amount and fee together before submitting; a valid-looking address alone does not confirm that it is the one you intended.

  4. Confirm the withdrawal using the exchange’s security checks, such as 2FA or email approval. Whitelisting an address can add a delay, and adding a new withdrawal address may trigger a temporary hold on many exchanges.

    Add and whitelist the wallet before you need the funds, especially if you expect to move ETH on a schedule. That gives any address review or hold time to run before the withdrawal is urgent. Once ETH is in your wallet on Ethereum, the Base bridge is a cross-chain service for moving supported assets between Ethereum and Base.

  5. Open the transaction hash provided by the exchange on Etherscan and follow the transfer. Each withdrawal gets a transaction hash that can be viewed there; once the transfer completes, the ETH balance should appear in your wallet on Ethereum.

The deciding detail is the network: choose Ethereum at the exchange and use the matching wallet address. When the ETH arrives, it is in your own wallet on Ethereum and ready for the next step, the Base bridge.
